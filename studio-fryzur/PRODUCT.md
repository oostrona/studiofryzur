## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user's choice, 2026-09-28). Deployable files in `site/`; everything else is development-only.

## Users

People from Czernica and the neighbouring villages of gmina Gaszowice (powiat rybnicki, Silesia), many of them long-time clients, who want a haircut, styling or hair for a big day (reviews mention a couple doing their wedding hair here). They book by phone; one review notes "trzeba zamówić termin". Many work shifts, which suits the salon's alternating morning and evening hours.

## Product Purpose

The website of Studio Fryzur, a hair salon at ul. Podleśna 21, 44-282 Czernica, run by Sylwia Durczok. It must make clear who they are, when they are open (the week alternates between morning and evening shifts), how to book (a phone call), and that clients have trusted them for decades.

## Positioning

A neighbourhood salon running since March 1992 (CEIDG registry start date; 34 years in 2026). Reviews repeat the same things: clients coming back for years, individual choice of haircut, advice on how to care for and style hair at home, a warm atmosphere, attractive prices, and two named stylists: Pani Sylwia (the owner, "szefowa") and Pani Dorota.

## Product Principles

1. The hours are the product's rhythm: make "when can I come" answerable at a glance.
2. Booking is one call away on every screen.
3. Trust comes from years and named people, not slogans.
4. No invented services, prices or photos.
